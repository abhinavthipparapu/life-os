# Product flows

**First run:** sign up, confirm the email, land on Home. Next steps point to creating a planner.

**Create a planner:** start blank, from a template, or from an AI draft. Blank drafts stay on the device until
saved. Templates become an editable copy. AI drafts stay previews until approved.

**Daily use:** open Today, tick things off, undo if needed. Finishing the day earns a short celebration, and
finishing a planner plays a one-time sequence. Offline, changes show Pending sync and send on reconnect.

**Review:** Analytics shows adherence with its definition. Dashboards hold user-built charts, and every chart
has a data table.
